APUSH Historical Thinking Skills

Argumentation

Every SAQ, LAQ, and DBQ requires a defensible thesis with supporting evidence. Practice: state claim + evidence + reasoning.

Contextualization

Connect the event to a broader historical development. Goes BEYOND the document or prompt — describe the "bigger picture."

CCOT

Continuity & Change Over Time: what stayed the same? What changed? Identify the turning points explicitly.

Causation

Short-term vs. long-term causes. Immediate triggers vs. underlying conditions. Effects: intended & unintended.

Comparison

Similarities AND differences. Use specific examples from both sides. Avoid generic statements.

Sourcing (DBQ)

For each document: Historical Situation · Audience · Purpose · Point of View. Use at least 3 documents for sourcing credit.
